How to read this
Suggested
The response expected to win the most points across the whole round. It uses each of your players once, and makes sure everyone can still play three games tonight.
Points
The points the suggested players are expected to win. Each player's likely score comes mostly from their IPR and how they score against the league on every machine, and more from their typical score on this one the more they've played it. Players score a little better at home, and on machines their team picked.
Few games
Your suggested players, or theirs, average fewer than 3 games on the machine, so their typical scores on it count for very little.
Or
The players next most likely to win points on their own, even if they're suggested for another game.
