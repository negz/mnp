How to read this
Suggested
The response expected to win the most points across the whole round. It uses each of your players once, and makes sure everyone can still play three games tonight.
Expect about
The points your suggested players are expected to win across the round. Each player's likely score comes mostly from their IPR and how they score against the league on every machine, and more from their typical score on this one the more they've played it. Players score a little better at home, and on machines their team picked.
To win
The chance your suggested players win the game. In doubles that means taking 3 or more of its 5 points.
Clear pick
Sending anyone else here would cost the round at least a quarter of a point. Most games cost the round less than that to change, so for those your own read of who's playing well tonight can decide.
Or
The players next most likely to win points on their own, even if they're suggested for another game.
