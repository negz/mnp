How to read this
Pick these
The machines, and who should play them, that win {{.Team1}} the most points if {{.Team2}} sends its best players against each. It's what mnp.tips would suggest {{.Team2}} send.
Likely to send
{{.Team2}}'s best response to all of the picks at once, using each of its players once.
Pinned
A game you've changed. It stays as you set it, and the other picks fit around it. Turn it off to let the suggestions change it again.
To win
The chance {{.Team1}}'s players win the game. In doubles that means taking 3 or more of its 5 points.
Machines
Untick any that are out of order tonight, or that you don't want to pick.
Players
Untick anyone who can't play this round, and add subs. Players who must play are always picked, for example because they sat out the last round. During a match this follows the lineups and who's played.
